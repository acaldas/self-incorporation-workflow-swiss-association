  **\[Association Name\] Regulation General Assembly** 

**Article I. Purpose and application** 

This Regulation General Assembly (**“Regulation GA”**) is adopted by the General Assembly of the 

**\[Association Name\]** association (**“Association"**) and governs 

together with the applicable laws, the **\[Association Name\]**  articles of association (as 

amended, the **"Articles"**), and other regulations (if any), the organization of the Association. 

In case of any conflict or inconsistency between the applicable laws, the Regulation GA, Articles or the 

an other regulation, the following order of precedence shall govern: (1) mandatory applicable laws, (2) 

the Articles and this Regulation GA (the more specific shall prevail), (3) the other regulations. In case of 

any conflict or inconsistency between the other regulations or organizational documents, the one relating 

to the subject matter shall prevail. 

**Article II. Definitions** 

Terms not otherwise defined in the Regulation GA shall have the meaning assigned to them in the 

Articles or any other regulation of the Association. 

**"Chair**" means the person elected by the General Assembly as chair, or, in the case of his inability, 

another Member attending the General Assembly. 

**"General Assembly**" means the meeting of the Members of the Association and the resolutions by means of written/electronic means *(Urabstimmung*). 

**"Member**" means a member of the Association as defined in the Articles. 

**"Chair**" means the member of the Board elected by the other members of the Board to preside over the 

Board. 

**Article III. General Membership Criteria** 

Applicants for membership in the Association must meet the following criteria: 

a. legally valid status as a party to a "Multisignature Participation Agreement" pertaining to a 

multisignature cryptocurrency wallet which is administered for the purpose of the Association as stated in Article 3 of the Articles. 

b. be a natural person (or persons), a decentralized unincorporated organization or an AI application 

in the Web3 industry represented by one or more natural or legal persons, corporation, partnership, association, trust, public or private non-profit research institution, governmental body, or other legal entity; 

c. willingness to support and work toward the purpose of the Association; 

d. good reputation; 

e. good standing; and 

f. no increased reputational, financial, or criminal risk for the Association by admitting the applicant. 

**Article IV. General Assembly** 

1\. **Convening and holding the General Assembly** 

The virtual or physical General Assembly will take place upon invitation by the Chair, or by request of a 

Member. The notice can be made electronically (e-mail or other means of communication) or in writing and 

shall state the place (if not a virtual assembly) and/or the access information (if a virtual assembly) and time 

of the meeting, the items of the agenda as well as the motions for consideration, if any. Meetings must be 

called at least five (5) days in advance. 

Alternative motions by Members for the agenda can be sent to the Chair within two (2) days after dispatch or 

publishing of the invitation notice with or without a reasoning. The Chair shall publish the alternative 

motions and any reasonings at least one (1) before the virtual or physical General Assembly. 

No later than ten (10) days prior to the day of the ordinary General Assembly (as set out in the notice calling 

the General Assembly), the financial statements of the Association and the report issued by the auditors shall 

be made available to the Members for inspection either at the registered office of the Association or online. 

If no objection is raised by any Member, the Members may hold a General Assembly without observing the 

prescribed formalities of calling the meeting. 

The General Assembly will be chaired by the Chair or, in the case of his inability, another member of the 

Board attending the General Assembly. 

2\. **Organisation of written/electronic resolutions ("Urabstimmungen")** 

Voting by written / electronic means (Urabstimmung) takes place upon determination by the Members and 

invitation by the Chair. The voting window shall be five (5) days and the deadline for submitting votes shall be communicated in the invitation. 

The invitation to participate in the written/ electronic voting has to be sent out or published at least five (5) days before the start of the voting window and can be made electronically (communication by mail, e-mail, or any other means of communication) or in writing. It shall include the items on which resolutions shall be taken as well as the motions as determined by the Board. 

In the case of a written / electronic voting (Urabstimmung) in lieu of an ordinary General Assembly the 

financial statements of the Association as well as the report issued by the auditors have to be disclosed online at the same time as the invitation is sent out by the Chair 

Alternative motions can be sent to the Board within two (2) days after dispatch or publishing of the voting material with or without reasoning. The Board shall publish the alternative motions and any reasonings at east one (1) day before the voting window starts together with the final voting material which allows the Members to vote on all motions received. 

3\. **Voting rights** 

Each Member has one vote at the General Assembly and for the written/ electronic voting (*Urabstimmung*). 

Votes are not transferable to another member or a third person. 

4\. **Majority requirements** 

Unless otherwise required by law or the Articles, resolutions shall be passed, and elections shall be made by the \[default majority rule \=absolute majority\]  of all votes cast at the General Assembly. 

The Chair of the meeting will not have the casting vote. 

The majorities for decisions of the General Assembly shall apply likewise to decisions taken by written / electronic communication *(Urabstimmung*). 

A Member and its Affiliates will be deemed as one (1) Member for all Association votes and quorum calculations. **"Affiliate"** or **"Affiliates"** means any entity that is controlled by, under common control with, or that controls the subject party; and 'control' means direct or indirect control of more than fifty (50) % of the voting power to elect directors of an entity or, for any other entity, the power to direct management of such entity. 

Any amendment of the Articles or these Regulations GA requires a \[special majority rule \= unanimous vote\] by all Members. 

**Article V. Modifications** 

The General Assembly may modify or amend any rule(s) in the Regulation GA at any time within the 

purpose of the Association for good governance, subject to any limitation in the Articles or any regulation.
