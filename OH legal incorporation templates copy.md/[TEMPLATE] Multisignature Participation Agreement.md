**MULTISIGNATURE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT** 

This Multisignature Participation Agreement (the " **Agreement** ") is being entered into as of date by and among the parties serving as Active Signers (as defined below). The parties hereto are persons sometimes referred to individually as a " **Party** " and collectively as the " **Parties** ." Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined shall have the respective meanings set forth in **Section 1** (Definitions). 

**BACKGROUND** 

1\. The Active Signers desire to enter into this Agreement to support the orderly administration and deployment of the Blockchain Tokens comprising the operations budget (the " **Designated Ops Budget** "). 

2\. In connection therewith, the Parties have established a security scheme to support the receipt, safeguarding, deployment, and audit of the Designated Ops Budget in accordance with the standards and procedures set forth in the Internal Policy and the Transactional Procedures whereby transactions are initiated and then executed in response to a transaction message that has been signed by at least decision\_Quorum private keys out of a set of keys\_Total private keys designated for such purpose (each private key in such set, a "**Key**") and broadcast to Ethereum for processing (such scheme, the "**Multisig Scheme**"). Accordingly, the Parties are entering into this Agreement to establish the rights, duties, procedures, and terms and conditions of the Parties’ participation in the Multisig Scheme.

3.7.1 **Vacancies in Active Signers**. Such person shall be designated a Party after the following: 

(i) a majority of the Active Signers provide written consent to such designation; and 

(ii) such person becomes a party to this Agreement by executing and delivering an additional counterpart signature page to this Agreement.   
Thereafter, such person shall be deemed be a party to and bound by this Agreement as a Party.

(i) a majority of the Active Signers provide written consent to such designation, 

(ii) if such person is not a Party, such person becomes a party to this Agreement by executing and delivering an additional counterpart signature page to this Agreement, thereafter such person shall be deemed to be a party to and bound by this Agreement as a Party.

In the event (i) a Vacancy is not filled pursuant to **Section 3.7.2** within the prescribed time or (ii) Vacancies pursuant to **Section 3.7.1** render the Multisig Scheme unable to execute transactions in the ordinary course, it shall constitute a termination event under **Section 4** and the remaining Parties to the Multisig Scheme shall execute a transaction to return any undeployed portion of the Designated Ops Budget to another wallet controlled by the Project or the party who funded the Multisig Scheme, as determined by the Parties.
