**6\.**

**Not *required to register in the Commercial Register*** 

*The Assembly notes that per January 1, 2023 according to article 61* (*2) CC associations in Switzerland are only required to register in the commercial register if the association (1) operates a business conducted in a commercial manner for its purpose, (2) is subject to auditing or (3) mainly collects or distributes assets abroad, directly or indirectly*, *intended for charitable religious, cultural, educational or social purposes*. 

*The Assembly acknowledges, resolves and confirms that the Association (1) does not self-finance its operation to fulfil its purpose by generating regular ongoing and high revenue through providing products or services against remuneration (operation of a business conducted in a commercial manner)*1 *and thus does not conducts a commercial operation in pursuit of its objects, (ii) it is not subject an audit requirement according to article 69b CC, and (iii) it does not does not directly or indirectly collect assets abroad that are not linked to a consideration (Gegenleistung) and thus does not primarily collect or distribute assets abroad, directly or indirectly, that are intended for charitable, religious, cultural*, *educational or social purpose Accordingly, the Association is not required to register in the commercial register according to article 61* (*2) CC.* 

*The Assembly will inform the competent commercial register and register if one of the requirements for the obligation to register according to article 61* (2*) CC are fulfilled in the future*.
